01

Complementary predictor adaptation

Decision supervision also updates the final TD-JEPA predictor block and projection. The adapted future offers a proposal to the calibrated composition rule.

Pretrained predictor→Final block + projection→Adapted future
See how the proposal is used +

Relational selection supplies the default action. The adapted predictor supplies a native-distance proposal. Calibrated composition admits it when the score advantage exceeds its threshold.

relational default → calibrated gate ← adapted proposal

Complementary predictive geometries

LeWM and TD-JEPA supply dense future–goal descriptors. JEPA-WM and DINO-WM add ordinal evidence in the four-geometry model.
